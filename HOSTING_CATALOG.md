# Fubrio Hosting

The Fubrio Hosting section is implemented in the public services area.

- The category can be expanded with the **Explore hosting** button.
- Each hosting service opens a plan and features dialog.
- The dialog includes a contact action that leads to the existing contact section.
- The editable `fubrio-hosting` service category is included in the Supabase schema and in `supabase/fubrio-hosting-category.sql` for existing databases.

The prices are displayed as provided in the requested catalog. Confirm infrastructure costs, resource limits, availability, taxes, backups, and support terms before accepting paid orders.
